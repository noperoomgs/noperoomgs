01 / OVERVIEW

Reconstruct the room.
Recover the perspective.

Indoor reconstruction gets difficult when visual cues disappear. Blank walls and abrupt camera motion can undermine camera tracking.

NopeRoomGS estimates poses and builds a Gaussian scene through local-to-global optimization, using tracking constraints and depth priors.

INPUT

Unposed images + known camera intrinsics
